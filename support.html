Restoring a purchase

If you've already bought DimmerPro Pro and it's not unlocked on a new Mac or after a reinstall, open the app's Paywall screen and tap Restore Purchases. This restores your one-time purchase directly from Apple — no account or login is required.

Free Trial

DimmerPro includes a 3-day free trial of all Pro features (multi-display sync, scheduler, up to 4 presets, global hotkey) that starts automatically the first time you open the app. Core screen dimming is always free and never requires a purchase.

Known system limitations

These are limits set by macOS itself, not bugs in DimmerPro:

  • DimmerPro cannot dim the lock screen — macOS does not allow App Store apps to draw above the login window layer.
  • DimmerPro cannot dim the mouse cursor — the system pointer renders on a hardware layer above every app window on macOS.
